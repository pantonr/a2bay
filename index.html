A2Bay

A Chrome extension that helps sellers and shoppers easily understand product relationships, variations, and family connections on Amazon.

Install from Chrome Web Store

Key Features

  • Instantly view all product variations and related items
  • See detailed product information including titles, descriptions, and bullet points
  • Compare conditions across variants
  • Export data to Google Sheets for analysis
  • View all associated images and videos

How to Use

  1. Visit any Amazon product page
  2. Click the A2Bay icon
  3. View comprehensive product information
  4. Export to Google Sheets if needed
