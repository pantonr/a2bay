Privacy Policy for A2Bay

Last updated: April 24, 2024

1. Information Collection

A2Bay collects:

  • Authentication information solely for Google Sheets integration
  • Product information from Amazon.com pages you visit while using the extension

2. Use of Information

We use collected information to:

  • Enable data export to Google Sheets
  • Display product relationships and variations
  • Store your preferences for extension functionality

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. No personal information is transmitted to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

We integrate with:

  • Google Sheets API (for data export functionality)
  • Amazon.com (to read product information)

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your information to third parties. We do not use data for any purpose unrelated to the extension's core functionality.

For privacy concerns, contact: philip@p-ant.com
